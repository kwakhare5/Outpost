# Outpost Glossary

A quick guide to the everyday words used across the Outpost app.

## Words We Use

**Dark Store**:
A small local fulfillment store in a neighborhood that packs and delivers orders in 10 minutes.
_Avoid_: Warehouse, depot, supermarket

**Main Warehouse**:
The big central warehouse outside the city that sends scheduled bulk orders to stores once or twice a day.
_Avoid_: Mother hub, distribution center

**Van Transfer**:
Moving extra stock from one local store to another in a small van when a store is running out of items fast.
_Avoid_: Inter-store transfer, IST, lateral reorder

**Sending Store**:
The store with extra stock that sends items to a store that is running out.
_Avoid_: Donor store, source hub

**Receiving Store**:
The store that is running out of stock and gets the van delivery.
_Avoid_: Target store, buyer hub

**Safety Cushion**:
The extra stock the sending store keeps so it doesn't run out of items itself after helping another store.
_Avoid_: Donor safety buffer, reserve threshold

**Hours Left**:
How many hours a store has before an item completely sells out.
_Avoid_: Time-to-stockout, TTS, burn rate

**Doorstep Count**:
Counting the boxes at the store door when the van pulls up to make sure nothing was lost on the road.
_Avoid_: GRN, goods received note, inwarding

**Damaged on the Road**:
Items that broke, leaked, or went missing during the van ride.
_Avoid_: Transit waste, transit shrink, inventory write-off

**Oldest First**:
Selling older batches before newer ones so food doesn't sit on the shelf and go bad.
_Avoid_: FEFO, FIFO, chronological batch dispatch

**Price Cut**:
Lowering the price on items expiring soon so customers buy them before they spoil.
_Avoid_: Markdown rescue, clearance promotion

**Expired Stock**:
Items that passed their expiration date and are locked so they can never be sold to a customer.
_Avoid_: Quarantined stock, unsellable inventory

**Time Clock**:
The button at the top of the screen that lets you move time forward by 1 hour to see how the day plays out.
_Avoid_: Simulation clock, virtual tick generator

**Without Outpost**:
A simple comparison that shows how much money and stock you would have lost if you did nothing.
_Avoid_: Paired baseline, control model

**Cart Rationing**:
Temporarily capping the maximum quantity a customer can purchase in a single order (e.g., max 2 cartons) to prevent bulk hoarding and keep stock available for more households during a shortage.
_Avoid_: Order throttling, quantity capping, purchase limit

**Operator Co-Pilot**:
A slide-out conversational assistant powered by Gemini that answers natural language questions about store status, stockout reasons, and what-if trade-offs using live database context.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, AI agent, virtual assistant
