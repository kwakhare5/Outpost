# Precomputed primary proof, out-of-sample upload proof

Status: accepted, 2026-11-17

The three real scores and the replay must be honest and comparable across datasets. The primary dataset's proof is produced by the offline study and seeded. An upload is scored on its own held-out last 30 days, or 20% of its history with a minimum of 7 days, using the fixed artifact. The assistant check runs per dataset. The replay runs only when stock, travel time, and prices exist. Both arms get every valid proposal approved by the same rule, and losses are shown.

Consequences: some datasets get reduced claims; every screen must label the active claims; demo and upload scores are never mixed.
