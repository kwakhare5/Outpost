# ADR 0001: Using Van Transfers Between Stores to Stop Stockouts

## What we decided
Our 3 dark stores (Andheri, Bandra, Powai) only hold 1 to 2 days of stock. The big warehouse outside Mumbai takes 6 to 10 hours to deliver orders, which is too slow when milk or bread suddenly sells out in the morning.

We decided to let stores send extra stock to each other using small local vans (like a Porter tempo) whenever the big warehouse truck cannot arrive in time.

## What this means
- Moving stock by van takes 40 to 60 minutes across Mumbai.
- The sending store must keep enough stock for itself so it doesn't run dry.
- The receiving store counts the boxes at the door to catch any leaking or broken cartons right away.
