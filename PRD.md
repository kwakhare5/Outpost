# PRD.md — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Executive Summary
Outpost is an all-day operations control room for a city inventory planner managing quick-commerce dark stores in Mumbai (Andheri West, Bandra, Powai). It spots store stockout and spoilage risks hours before they happen, recommends the best fix (van transfer, warehouse reorder, price cut, or cart rationing), and waits for the human planner to hit **Approve** before any real inventory or money moves.

All customer demand curves, hourly sales burn rates, and baseline comparisons are grounded directly in the real-world `FreshRetailNet-50K` dark store dataset.

---

## 2. Target User & Core Problem

* **Target User:** Karan, the City Inventory & Replenishment Planner, controlling all 3 dark stores across Mumbai: **Andheri West**, **Bandra**, and **Powai**.
* **Core Product Catalog (5 Anchor Staples):**
  1. **Amul Taaza Milk 500ml** (MRP ₹27) — Hero stockout staple; high morning breakfast velocity.
  2. **Mother Dairy Classic Dahi 400g** (MRP ₹35) — Hero expiry staple; short 4-day shelf life.
  3. **Harvest Gold White Bread 400g** (MRP ₹45) — Morning rush companion staple.
  4. **Fresh Farm Eggs 6-pack** (MRP ₹60) — Morning high-volume breakfast protein.
  5. **Amul Salted Butter 100g** (MRP ₹58) — Steady baseline refrigerator staple.
* **Core Problem:**
  * In 10-minute grocery delivery, dark stores carry only 1 to 2 days of inventory due to limited shelf space (2,500–4,000 sq ft).
  * Fast-moving staples (milk, bread, eggs) experience sharp morning surges (06:00 to 10:00 AM) that deplete stock unexpectedly.
  * Central warehouse replenishment trucks take 6 to 10 hours from Bhiwandi to reach city stores due to loading queues and highway traffic, arriving far too late to stop a morning stockout.
  * At the same time, short-life dairy products (dahi, fresh milk) expire on shelves and turn into 100% financial waste if not sold quickly.
* **User Outcome:**
  * Karan monitors one live screen throughout the day, sees the top urgent risks ranked by **"Hours Left"**, reviews the proof and plain-English Gemini explanations, approves or adjusts recommendations with one click, watches delivery vans travel in real time, confirms the doorstep count, and reviews the honest rupee impact backed by real dark store data.

## 3. Core Features (v1 Scope)

### 1. Live Command Queue (`design/screen-1-main.png`)
* **User Flow:** 
  * Karan views a single active command screen displaying all 3 stores, with cards prioritized strictly by **Hours Left** (what will run out first).
  * Karan clicks the top risk: **Amul Taaza Milk 500ml** at **Andheri West** (projected stockout in ~50 minutes, 38 units on hand, burning 45 units/hour during the morning breakfast rush).
  * The proof pane displays: current stock (38 units), the real hourly sales curve from `FreshRetailNet`, why incoming warehouse truck PO-4471 is arriving too late (18:40), and the recommended fix: **Van Transfer of 40 units from Bandra** (Bandra has 112 units on hand and only needs 60 for the day).
  * Karan reads the plain-English explanation generated by Gemini, optionally adjusts the transfer quantity (e.g. adjusts 40 to 35 cartons), and clicks **Approve** (or **Reject**).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Queue ranks cards strictly by time-to-empty across all 3 stores.
  * Sending store safety cushion is enforced: Bandra must retain enough stock for its own projected demand plus a 20% safety buffer; transfers exceeding this threshold are blocked.
  * Approving generates a persisted transfer record with a unique UUID.
  * Rejecting closes the recommendation or displays at most one feasible alternative without spamming duplicate alerts.

### 2. In-Flight Van Tracking & Doorstep Count Confirmation (`design/screen-2-inflight.png`)
* **User Flow:**
  * After approval, the transfer appears on the In-Flight screen with milestone progress: `Approved → Picked → Dispatched → In Transit → Awaiting Count Confirmation → Received`.
  * The van travels along the Mumbai transit corridor from Bandra to Andheri West (~45 minutes).
  * When the van arrives at Andheri, the doorstep count input unlocks. The store receiver counts the physical cartons at the shutter door and enters the count (e.g. 38 undamaged units received, 2 broken cartons).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * **Conservation of Mass:** Dispatching immediately deducts 40 units from Bandra.
  * **No Teleportation:** Andheri stock receives **zero units** during transit. Units are credited ONLY when the doorstep count is confirmed.
  * **Road Damage Accounting:** If 40 were dispatched but 38 arrive intact, 38 units are credited to sellable stock and 2 units are logged directly to "Damaged on the Road" in the financial loss ledger.
  * Doorstep count confirmation is completely idempotent (no double-crediting on page reload or repeated submissions).

### 3. Price Cuts & Spoilage Rescue
* **User Flow:**
  * The system spots items with batches expiring within 24 hours (e.g. Mother Dairy Dahi 400g) where normal demand will not clear the shelf before expiration.
  * It recommends a **Price Cut (e.g. 30% off, dropping from ₹35 to ₹25)** to accelerate sales velocity.
  * The recommendation card displays clear rupee trade-offs:
    * *Do nothing:* 20 cups spoil → lose ₹700 (100% write-off).
    * *Apply 30% discount:* sell 18 cups at ₹25 → recover ₹450 in cash (net benefit of ₹450 vs ₹0).
  * Karan approves the price cut; selling price updates, and customer orders clear the stock.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Alerts trigger strictly based on batch expiration timestamps.
  * **Oldest First:** Orders always consume the oldest batch first.
  * **Zero Expired Food:** Any batch that reaches its expiration timestamp without being sold is automatically locked, deducted from available inventory, and logged directly to the waste ledger. Expired stock can never be fulfilled to a customer.

### 4. Realized Outcomes & Real Baseline Comparison (`design/screen-3-outcomes.png`)
* **User Flow:**
  * Karan opens the Outcomes tab to review the financial and operational score of the day.
  * Sees the 4 core plain-English metrics:
    1. **Lost Sales Units:** Items customers wanted that were out of stock.
    2. **Damaged Stock Units:** Cartons damaged in transit or expired on shelves.
    3. **Rupee Loss:** Total money lost `(Lost Sales + Damaged Stock) * Unit Price`.
    4. **Availability %:** Percentage of customer demand successfully fulfilled.
  * Reviews the honest side-by-side comparison: **"With Outpost"** vs **"Without Outpost" (Industry Baseline)**.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Customer demand streams and baseline numbers are grounded in the real `FreshRetailNet-50K` dark store dataset.
  * "Without Outpost" accurately simulates what would have happened if no van transfer was made: shelves running dry at 08:50 AM, staying empty until 18:40 PM, and losing all intermediate customer orders.
  * Rupee savings reflect actual prevented lost sales minus transit damage and discount costs.
  * Delays, damaged stock, and underperformance are displayed honestly.

### 5. Top-Bar Time Clock Controller
* **User Flow:**
  * The top bar features an operational clock (e.g. `08:00 AM`) with an **"Advance 1 Hour"** button.
  * Clicking "Advance 1 Hour" advances the simulation clock by 60 minutes:
    * Consumes customer orders based on the hourly sales curve.
    * Deducts inventory using the Oldest-First rule.
    * Advances active vans down their transit routes.
    * Recalculates "Hours Left" for all items.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * The clock is deterministic and reproducible.
  * Allows a complete day's operational narrative (from morning milk warning to doorstep arrival and evening outcomes) to be walked through in 2 minutes.

### 6. Edge Case Handling Rules
* **Edge Case 1: When no local store has surplus stock:**
  * If neither Bandra nor Powai has enough stock to spare without violating their Safety Cushion, Outpost blocks van transfers.
  * Instead, it triggers **Cart Rationing** (limiting app orders to a maximum of 1 or 2 cartons per customer) to stretch remaining inventory across households until the warehouse truck arrives, and flags PO-4471 for priority dock receiving.
* **Edge Case 2: When two stores run out at the exact same time:**
  * If both Andheri and Powai are projected to run dry at the same hour, Outpost scores priority by **Hourly Sales Velocity at Risk (₹/minute)**: the store burning through cash faster gets the van first.
  * If the sending store has enough surplus, Outpost recommends a proportional split transfer to keep both stores afloat.

### 7. Outpost Co-Pilot (Conversational Operator Assistant)
* **User Flow:**
  * Karan clicks the chat icon in the bottom-right corner to open a slide-out drawer.
  * Asks natural language questions:
    * *"What is the current status of Bandra?"*
    * *"Why didn't we transfer milk from Powai instead?"*
    * *"What happens if we do nothing about Andheri?"*
  * The Co-Pilot queries live Supabase tables, feeds the factual context to Gemini, and answers in 2–3 plain, concise sentences.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * **Advisory Only:** Co-Pilot explains and answers; it is strictly forbidden from executing van transfers or changing database state directly.
  * **Factually Grounded:** Every answer uses live stock, batch, and transit rows from Supabase (zero hallucinations).
  * **Zero Crashing:** Pre-computed deterministic fallback responses are returned if offline or if the API key is unavailable.

### 8. Custom CSV Data Importer & Engine Evaluator
* **User Flow:**
  * A quick-commerce VP, engineer, or manager clicks **"Import Store CSV"** in the top bar.
  * Uploads their own dark store data containing: `store_name`, `product_name`, `current_stock`, `hourly_sales_rate`, `unit_mrp`, `expiry_hours`.
  * The backend validates the columns, ingests the data into Supabase, and reconfigures the entire control room around **their stores and their products**.
  * The manager immediately sees stockout forecasts, van transfer recommendations, and rupee outcomes calculated live on their custom data.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Validates required columns and rejects malformed files with clear inline errors.
  * Successfully creates store, product, and batch rows in Supabase.
  * The Urgency Engine immediately runs on the uploaded inventory, generating accurate "Hours Left" alerts and transfer suggestions for the custom stores.
  * Allows one-click reset back to the default Mumbai benchmark dataset.

## 4. What We Are NOT Building (Strict Out of Scope)

* **No consumer grocery shopping app:** Customers do not browse or place orders through Outpost.
* **No driver GPS tracking / map engine:** We track operational milestones and transit times, not live turn-by-turn road maps.
* **No autonomous black-box ordering:** No purchase orders or transfers execute without verified human approval.
* **No fake undo button:** Real dispatches cannot be undone once the van is in transit.
* **No multiple shift modes or screen switching:** Outpost is a single, continuous command screen active all day.
* **No invented or made-up customer numbers:** Default curves derive from real dark store data; custom curves come from user CSV.

---

## 5. Hard Invariants (Non-Negotiable)

1. **The Plain Language Standard:** Zero textbook jargon, zero academic acronyms (no FEFO, FIFO, IST, GRN, TTS, or complex buzzwords). Every single screen label, button, metric, alert, and explanation must be written in simple, everyday conversational English that an 8th grader understands in one read.
2. **Conservation of Mass:** Dispatched stock deducts from the sending store immediately and credits zero units to the destination during transit.
3. **Doorstep Physical Truth:** Stock is credited only upon manual physical count at the door; discrepancies are logged directly to damaged waste.
4. **Level-2 Autonomy:** Zero interventions execute without verified human approval.
5. **Real Database Calls Only:** Zero mocking of project code or databases; every number on screen comes from Supabase PostgreSQL.

## 6. Success Metrics & Done State

* **The Milk Test:** An interviewer or reviewer can boot the app, follow one carton of Amul Milk from an 08:00 AM stockout warning, approve a 40-unit Bandra transfer, advance the clock, confirm arrival at Andheri West (with 2 broken cartons logged to road damage), and see the exact savings on Outcomes compared to doing nothing.
* **The External Verification Test:** A dark store manager can upload their own store CSV and watch the engine compute real stockout alerts and transfer recommendations for their stores.
* **100% Real API:** No canned or mocked JSON on the frontend; every number on screen comes from the backend Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL database.
* **Zero Jargon:** All screen labels and metrics use plain everyday words (Lost Sales, Damaged Stock, Rupee Loss, Availability %, Hours Left, Doorstep Count).
* **Terminal Proof:** Types check clean, build succeeds, and all automated tests exit 0 via `.\verify.ps1`.
