# PRD.md — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Executive Summary
Outpost is an operations control room for a city inventory planner managing quick-commerce dark stores in Mumbai. It spots store stockout and spoilage risks hours before they happen, recommends the best fix (inter-store transfer, warehouse reorder, or markdown discount), and waits for the human manager to hit **Approve** before any real inventory or money moves.

---

## 2. Target User & Core Problem

* **Target User:** Karan, the City Inventory & Replenishment Planner, who controls all 3 dark stores across Mumbai: **Andheri West**, **Bandra**, and **Powai**.
* **Core Problem:**
  * In 10-minute delivery, everyday essentials (milk, bread, eggs) sell out rapidly, leaving customers stranded and losing sales.
  * At the same time, fresh perishable items (dahi, milk) sit on shelves and spoil, turning into pure financial waste.
  * Today, planners either react too late after shelves are already empty, or get spammed with unexplainable, complex automated alerts.
* **User Outcome:**
  * Karan opens one dashboard, sees the top urgent fires for the morning, reviews the evidence and costs in plain English, approves or rejects recommendations with one click, watches the delivery van travel in real time, confirms the arrival count at the door, and sees the real rupee impact.

## 3. Core Features (v1 Scope)

### 1. Morning Briefing & Urgency Queue (`design/screen-1-main.png`)
* **User Flow:** 
  * Karan logs in at 08:00 AM and sees the morning briefing with 4 summary tiles: urgent fires, late vans, expiry risks, and routine orders auto-handled.
  * The queue is sorted by what will run out first. Karan clicks the top risk: **Amul Taaza Milk 500ml** at **Andheri West** (projected stockout in ~5 hours).
  * The right pane shows the proof: current stock (38 units), demand forecast curve, why incoming warehouse PO-4471 is arriving too late (18:40), and the recommended fix: transfer 40 units from Bandra (which has 112 units on hand and only needs 60).
  * Karan reads the plain-English explanation generated by Gemini and clicks **Approve** (or **Reject**).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Hero milk risk displays ~5 hours before stockout.
  * Bandra donor validation proves surplus without causing a secondary shortage.
  * Clicking Approve generates a persisted shipment with a unique recommendation UUID.
  * Rejecting closes the decision chain or offers at most one feasible backup.

### 2. In-Flight Tracking & Doorstep Count Gate (`design/screen-2-inflight.png`)
* **User Flow:**
  * After approval, the transfer appears on the In-Flight screen with step-by-step transit progress: `Approved → Picked → Dispatched → In Transit → Awaiting Count Confirmation → Received`.
  * As time advances, the van travels from Bandra to Andheri West.
  * When the van arrives at Andheri, the arrival confirmation unlocks. Karan or the store receiver confirms the counted units delivered.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * **Conservation of Mass:** Dispatching immediately deducts 40 units from Bandra.
  * **No Teleportation:** Andheri stock receives **zero units** during transit. Units are credited ONLY when arrival count confirmation is committed.
  * **Discrepancy Handling:** If 40 were dispatched but 38 arrive undamaged, 38 units are credited to Andheri stock and 2 units are logged to discrepancy waste.
  * Arrival confirmation is completely idempotent (no double-crediting on page reload or repeated clicks).

### 3. Markdown / Expiry Rescue
* **User Flow:**
  * The system spots items with heavy stock expiring in 24 hours (e.g. Mother Dairy Dahi) where normal demand won't sell them in time.
  * It recommends a price markdown (e.g. 30% discount) to accelerate sales velocity.
  * Karan approves the markdown; the selling price drops, customers buy up the stock, and the store recovers revenue instead of writing off 100% loss in the trash.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Alerts trigger strictly based on batch expiry timestamps.
  * Clear calculation showing expected waste avoided vs discount cost.
  * FIFO/FEFO is strictly enforced: oldest batches are sold first, and expired units are automatically quarantined and can never be fulfilled.

### 4. Measured Outcomes & Honest Baseline Comparison (`design/screen-3-outcomes.png`)
* **User Flow:**
  * Karan switches to the Outcomes tab to evaluate how the day went.
  * Sees the 4 core plain-language metrics:
    1. **Lost Sales Units:** Items customers wanted that we couldn't deliver.
    2. **Wasted Stock Units:** Expired or damaged units thrown away.
    3. **Rupee Loss:** Total money lost `(Lost Sales + Wasted Stock) * Unit Price`.
    4. **Availability %:** Percentage of customer demand successfully fulfilled.
  * Reviews the "Measured vs Expected" table showing what happened for each decision (including failures and worse-than-expected outcomes).
  * Compares today's results against a simple routine reorder baseline run on the exact same seed to verify real improvement.
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Every single number is derived directly from database events, not hardcoded constants.
  * Underperformance, delays, and negative outcomes are shown honestly.
  * Paired baseline comparison uses the identical demand stream and seed.

### 5. Plain-English AI Explanation (Gemini)
* **User Flow:**
  * Karan views any recommendation or what-if scenario.
  * Gemini translates the raw inventory numbers into simple, conversational explanations (e.g. *"Bandra has 112 milks and only needs 60 today, so sending 40 to Andheri stops a stockout with zero risk to Bandra."*).
* **Acceptance Criteria:**
  * Gemini explains and simulates; it NEVER places orders or executes interventions on its own.
  * If the LLM is unavailable, pre-computed fallback explanations are displayed clearly.

## 4. What We Are NOT Building (Strict Out of Scope)

* **No consumer grocery shopping app:** Customers do not browse or order through Outpost.
* **No driver GPS tracking / map engine:** We track milestones and delivery times, not live street maps.
* **No black-box autonomous ordering:** No purchase orders or transfers execute without human approval.
* **No fake CSV upload modal:** The non-functional CSV button and modal are removed.
* **No fake undo button:** Real physical dispatches cannot be magically undone once the truck is on the road.
* **No SaaS billing or multi-user auth:** Outpost is a focused, single-operator command center for Karan.

---

## 5. Success Metrics & Done State

* **The Milk Test:** An interviewer or reviewer can boot the app, follow one carton of Amul Milk from an 8:00 AM stockout warning, approve a Bandra transfer, advance time, confirm arrival at Andheri West, and see the exact savings on Outcomes.
* **100% Real API:** No canned or mocked JSON on the frontend; every number on screen comes from the backend SQLite database.
* **Zero Jargon:** All screen labels and metrics are plain English (Lost Sales, Wasted Stock, Rupee Loss, Availability %).
* **Terminal Proof:** All backend tests pass, Next.js build succeeds, and lint exits 0.
